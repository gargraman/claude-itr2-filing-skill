# Document Checklist — ITR-2 for AY 2026-27 (FY 2025-26)

Every document below is either required to fill a schedule accurately or to defend a claim in scrutiny (Sec 143(1)/(2)/(3)). Preserve for **8 years** from end of AY (Sec 149 reassessment window is 5 years for regular; longer for Black Money Act cases).

Mark each item **AVAILABLE**, **NEEDS INPUT**, or **NOT APPLICABLE** during Step 1 of the SKILL.md workflow.

## I. AIS/26AS/TIS reconciliation

Non-negotiable pre-filing step. Download all three:

- **Form 26AS** — `https://www.incometax.gov.in` → Services → View 26AS (redirects to TRACES)
- **AIS** — `https://www.incometax.gov.in` → Services → Annual Information Statement (redirects to Compliance Portal)
- **TIS** — same AIS page — Taxpayer Information Summary link

**26AS:** authoritative for TDS/TCS/advance-tax/self-assessment tax/refund. Every entry in Schedule 20 must match 26AS.

**AIS:** authoritative for income completeness. Every SFT-* entry (SFT-005 interest coop, SFT-016 dividend, SFT-018 salary, SFT-004 credit card, SFT-011 mutual fund, SFT-012 real estate, LRS outward remittance, TDS by tenant u/s 194IB, etc.) must be reflected in ITR-2 or explained via AIS Feedback.

**Any mismatch → resolve BEFORE filing** — either update ITR to match, or submit AIS Feedback with correction (accept/reject/duplicate). CPC uses AIS for automated 143(1) reconciliation.

## J. Miscellaneous safety documents
